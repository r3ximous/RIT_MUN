Code of Conduct

The standards every delegate agrees to during the conference.

On this page

  1. Respect
  2. Diplomatic conduct
  3. Attendance and punctuality
  4. Dress
  5. Academic integrity
  6. Raising a concern

Draft. This page is a draft based on standard Model UN practice. The Secretariat will publish the official version before the conference.

Respect

Every delegate, chair, staff member and guest is entitled to be treated with respect. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks have no place at the conference, in committee or outside it.

Diplomatic conduct

  • Address the chair and other delegates formally during debate.
  • Represent your assigned country’s policy faithfully, even where you personally disagree with it.
  • Disagree with arguments, not with people.

Attendance and punctuality

Delegates are expected to attend every committee session and to arrive on time. Tell your chair if you need to miss part of a session.

Dress

Business attire is the standard at Model UN conferences. Specific guidance for this conference will be confirmed by the Secretariat.

Academic integrity

Speeches, position papers and resolutions must be your own work. Pre-written resolutions are not permitted.

Raising a concern

If something makes you uncomfortable or you see a breach of this code, speak to your chair or any member of the Secretariat, or write to the Secretariat by email. Concerns are handled discreetly.
