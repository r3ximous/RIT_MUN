Frequently asked questions

Who can attend RIT Dubai MUN?

The conference is open to high school and university students. Beginners to advanced delegates are welcome to participate.

I have never done Model UN. Can I still take part?

Yes. The World Health Organization committee is set at beginner level, and the delegate guides on this site explain the procedure before you arrive.

Are position papers mandatory?

To be considered for an award, yes. All delegates must submit a position paper by November 1, 2026.

How do I register?

Registration is through the RIT Dubai MUN registration form. You will find the link on the Registration page of this site as soon as the form opens.
