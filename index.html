A conference built for young diplomats

Join hundreds of delegates at RIT Dubai for a weekend of rigorous debate, negotiation and global problem-solving.

Open to high school and university students. Beginners to advanced delegates are welcome.

New to Model UN? Start with the delegate guides

Letter from the Secretary-General

Draft placeholder: to be replaced with the Secretary-General’s own letter

Dear delegates,

Welcome to the RIT Dubai Model United Nations. Over three days at our campus in Dubai Silicon Oasis, you will debate the questions that real diplomats are still working to answer, from cyber security to access to essential medicines.

Whether this is your first conference or your tenth, our committees are designed to challenge you and to help you grow as a speaker, negotiator and writer. Our chairs and Secretariat will support you at every step.

We look forward to welcoming you to RIT Dubai in December.

Rex MarcianoSecretary-General, RIT Dubai Model United Nations
