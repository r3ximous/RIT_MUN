Registration includes access to all committee sessions, the delegate social and conference materials.

The registration form opens soon

Registration is through the RIT Dubai MUN registration form. Follow @ritd.mun on Instagram (opens in a new tab) for the announcement.

How to register

  1. Fill in the RIT Dubai MUN registration form, as an individual delegate or as part of a school delegation.
  2. Pay your registration fee, following the instructions the Secretariat sends you.
  3. Receive your committee and country allocation from the Secretariat.
  4. Research your country and submit your position paper by November 1, 2026.

An outline of the process. Exact steps will be confirmed when the registration form opens.
