Committees

Four committees, each designed to challenge delegates on pressing global issues, from first-timer friendly to full crisis.

  • UNSC

    United Nations Security Council

    CrisisAdvancedSmall committee

    Tackling immediate threats to international peace and security, with dynamic crisis updates.

    • Cyberwarfare & International Security
    • The Situation in the South China Sea
    View committee

  • WHO

    World Health Organization

    SpecializedBeginnerLarge committee

    Coordinating international responses to emerging global health pandemics.

    • Global Pandemic Preparedness
    • Access to Essential Medicines in Developing Nations
    View committee

  • UNHRC

    United Nations Human Rights Council

    General AssemblyIntermediateMedium committee

    Addressing systemic human rights violations and establishing global protection frameworks.

    • Protection of Refugees
    • Freedom of Expression in the Digital Age
    View committee

  • DISEC

    Disarmament and International Security Committee

    General AssemblyIntermediateLarge committee

    Focusing on cyber warfare, arms control and international security.

    • Regulation of Autonomous Weapons
    • Nuclear Proliferation in the Middle East
    View committee

Choosing a committee

  • Beginner

    A good first committee. The pace leaves room to learn the procedure as you go, and no previous Model UN experience is expected.

    WHO

  • Intermediate

    Best if you have attended at least one conference and are comfortable giving speeches and drafting clauses.

    UNHRC, DISEC

  • Advanced

    Recommended for experienced delegates who are confident with procedure and can adapt quickly as the situation changes.

    UNSC
